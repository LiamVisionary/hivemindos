# Small Business Preview Engine

Find local businesses that could benefit from a better website and build respectful, personalized previews.

## Contract

This skill is for consent-aware prospecting and concept work. Do not scrape at scale, bypass rate limits, harvest personal data, spam businesses, or claim affiliation. Use public business information and keep outreach honest.

## Inputs

- Location, category, target business size, and excluded categories.
- Definition of a good fit: missing website, broken site, poor mobile UX, outdated menu, weak booking flow, bad map/email links, or strong reviews with poor conversion.
- Output target: lead list, preview site, outreach copy, dashboard, or Work Board tasks.

## Workflow

1. Confirm scope and allowed data sources.
2. Discover candidates using approved tools: user-provided list, search, maps, local-business scraper if installed and approved, or manual review.
3. Score each candidate:
   - public rating/review count
   - site availability and mobile quality
   - conversion blockers
   - obvious business value of a better site
   - contact surface quality
4. Build one preview concept per approved candidate. Use actual public facts, not invented testimonials, claims, or offers.
5. Package previews with a plain-language note explaining what was improved and that it is an unsolicited concept.
6. Ask before sending outreach or publishing previews.

## Capability Map

- Lead discovery: `local-business-scraper` only when installed and user-approved, otherwise browser/search/manual review.
- Website generation: frontend/design skills and local static output.
- Outreach: messaging/email provider only after explicit approval and provider receipt.
- Tracking: use Work Board tasks for each prospect or campaign step.

## Safety

- Do not use private browser profiles.
- Do not email or message businesses without approval.
- Do not imply the preview is an official site.
- Remove or anonymize lead data on request.
