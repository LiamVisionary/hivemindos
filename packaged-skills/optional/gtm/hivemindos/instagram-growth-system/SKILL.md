# Instagram Growth System

Turn an Instagram account, niche, audience, and offer into one connected research, content, conversion, and measurement loop.

## Core Contract

- Match the requested scope. If the user asks for one hook, audit, idea bank, or repurposing pass, deliver that component without forcing the full system.
- Ground account-specific recommendations in public posts, user-provided exports, screenshots, links, or first-party analytics. Label unavailable evidence and assumptions.
- Browse current sources for claims about recent trends, platform behavior, competitors, or audience conversations when tools are available. Treat visible public metrics as directional.
- Optimize for qualified attention and business outcomes, not follower count alone. Never guarantee reach, virality, leads, or sales.
- Draft and analyze by default. Do not post, schedule, DM, comment, follow, buy promotion, or change an account without explicit approval and a suitable execution workflow.

## Input Brief

Collect only details that change the result:

- Account handle or profile URL, niche, and current positioning.
- Primary audience and the problem or transformation they care about.
- Offer, conversion path, and business goal, if monetization matters.
- Current cadence, formats, production capacity, and time budget.
- Brand voice, proof assets, boundaries, and topics to avoid.
- First-party baseline: reach, watch time, saves, shares, follows, profile visits, link clicks, leads, and sales when available.

If the user wants speed, state reasonable assumptions and continue.

## Evidence Pass

Build the strategy from three evidence layers:

1. **Account baseline**
   - Review up to 30 recent accessible posts and Reels.
   - Record date, topic, format, opening, CTA, visible performance, and comment themes.
   - Compare winners with the account median rather than using absolute views alone.
2. **Audience reality**
   - Use first-party research, customer calls, support logs, reviews, public conversations, search behavior, and competitor comments when permitted.
   - Separate observed language from inferred motivations.
3. **Market opportunity**
   - Identify overused themes, unanswered questions, weak competitor positions, format gaps, and timely conversations.
   - Mark each finding `confirmed`, `directional`, or `hypothesis`.

If evidence is missing, return a research plan plus a provisional strategy instead of inventing facts.

## Eight-Module Workflow

### 1. Growth Blueprint

Define:

- One-sentence position and audience promise.
- Primary audience, secondary audience, and explicit non-audience.
- Three to five content pillars, each tied to an audience need and business objective.
- A format mix across Reels, carousels, single-image posts, Stories, Lives, and collaborations.
- Discovery, trust, and conversion themes.
- Cadence that fits the creator's real production capacity.
- Profile, pinned-post, Highlights, and CTA improvements when evidence supports them.

Explain which behaviors the plan intends to change: stop, watch, save, share, follow, visit, reply, subscribe, inquire, or buy.

### 2. Audience Research Engine

Create an audience map covering:

- Frustrations and costly current-state problems.
- Desired outcomes and identity goals.
- Fears, objections, risks, and failed alternatives.
- Buying triggers, proof requirements, and reasons to delay.
- Exact observed phrases with source links when quoting public evidence.
- Content implications: themes, angles, formats, proof, and CTAs.

Keep observations and interpretations in separate columns so inference does not masquerade as research.

### 3. Content Idea Engine

Generate the requested number of ideas; use 50 for a full bank.

Distribute ideas across:

- Mistakes.
- Myths.
- Contrarian or unpopular positions.
- Lessons and behind-the-scenes proof.
- Frameworks and step-by-step education.
- Hidden opportunities.
- Timely trends.
- Specific pains, objections, and desired outcomes.

For each idea, include the audience tension, format, opening angle, value promise, proof needed, CTA, funnel stage, and why it fits the account. Reject vague ideas that could apply to any niche.

### 4. Hook And Reel Builder

Turn a selected idea into a retention-aware Reel:

- Open with a specific tension, outcome, contradiction, or unanswered question.
- Establish relevance immediately.
- Deliver concise beats with a clear progression.
- End with a useful takeaway and one proportionate CTA.
- Add visual beats, on-screen text, cuts, or proof frames when production detail is useful.

Keep the language conversational and natural to say aloud. Avoid empty urgency, bait-and-switch hooks, and unsupported claims.

### 5. Content Optimizer

Audit existing content for:

- Weak or delayed opening.
- Generic phrasing.
- Unclear audience or position.
- Repetition, filler, and slow pacing.
- Missing proof or specificity.
- Format mismatch.
- Confusing or oversized CTA.

Return a concise diagnosis, a revised version that preserves the core message, and a short change log explaining why the revision should improve clarity, retention, or perceived value.

### 6. Sales Content Engine

Generate the requested number of conversion-oriented ideas; use 20 for a full bank.

Balance:

- Problem education.
- Objection handling.
- Cost-of-inaction.
- Method or mechanism.
- Proof and demonstrations.
- Comparison and decision support.
- Transformation and use cases.
- Direct offer explanation.

Make the content useful without requiring the purchase. Position the offer as a plausible next step, not an inevitable or guaranteed solution.

### 7. Repurposing Engine

Turn one proven source idea into channel-shaped assets:

- Reel or short video.
- Carousel.
- Feed caption.
- Story sequence.
- Thread.
- Short spoken script.

Preserve the central claim and proof, but rebuild the opening, pacing, information density, CTA, and visual treatment for each format. Do not merely crop or paste the same copy everywhere.

### 8. Sixty-Day Operating System

Build a practical solo-creator plan in four phases:

1. **Days 1-10 — baseline and position:** audit the account, validate the audience, clarify the offer and profile, and establish the scoreboard.
2. **Days 11-25 — controlled production:** publish across the chosen pillars and formats, test distinct hooks, and record comparable results.
3. **Days 26-45 — repeat winners:** expand the strongest topic-format-hook combinations, add repurposing, and introduce trust and conversion content.
4. **Days 46-60 — conversion and systemization:** tighten CTAs and offer paths, document the weekly workflow, stop weak patterns, and plan the next cycle from evidence.

For every week, provide:

- Objectives.
- Content slots and formats.
- Production batch.
- Engagement workflow.
- Conversion action.
- Metrics review.
- Keep, revise, or stop decision.

## Scoreboard

Choose metrics that match the goal:

- Discovery: non-follower reach, three-second hold, average watch time, completion, shares.
- Value: saves, meaningful comments, repeat viewers, Story completion.
- Audience: qualified follows, profile visits, follower-to-profile conversion.
- Pipeline: replies, DMs, email signups, calls, trials, qualified leads.
- Revenue: attributed customers, conversion rate, contribution margin, payback when paid promotion exists.

Compare like with like: format, topic, audience, post age, and distribution conditions. Avoid declaring winners from one post or incomplete-day data.

## Capability Handoffs

- Use `instagram-reel-growth-workflow` when the work centers on public-account pattern analysis, current short-form signal, or production-ready Reel scripting.
- Use `organic-reach-gtm` when the Instagram work belongs inside a broader B2B content-to-pipeline system.
- Use a dedicated media workflow for asset generation, assembly, subtitles, or render QA.
- Use a dedicated social publishing workflow only after the user explicitly asks to publish or schedule. Require platform receipts before reporting success.

## Quality And Safety

- Never fabricate analytics, customer language, testimonials, results, competitors, or platform rules.
- Do not scrape private accounts, bypass login walls, expose personal data, or automate spammy engagement.
- Keep claims truthful and identify disclosures required for paid, affiliate, sponsored, or gifted content.
- Prefer a sustainable cadence with a learning loop over arbitrary daily volume.
- Preserve the user's voice and original point of view; do not clone another creator's exact content or identity.
