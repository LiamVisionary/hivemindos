# Daily Briefing Trailer

Turn a real day into a short cinematic briefing artifact.

## Contract

The trailer is a briefing format, not a substitute for reading private inboxes or calendars without permission. Use connected personal data only when the user has granted access in the current environment.

## Inputs

- Date or time range.
- Allowed sources: calendar, email summary, Slack/export, Work Board, notes, or user-pasted agenda.
- Tone: cinematic, funny, calm, urgent, premium, playful, or plain.
- Output: script, storyboard, generated clips, assembled video, or HTML preview.

## Workflow

1. Gather the day plan from approved sources. Mark inaccessible or missing sources.
2. Identify the three narrative beats: stakes, obstacles, and payoff.
3. Write a 20 to 40 second trailer beat board with text-on-screen lines, no private details in public frames unless approved.
4. Create shot prompts with a consistent subject/environment only when visual generation is requested.
5. Generate or assemble clips through discovered video tools, then verify video duration, nonblank frames, readable text, and audio/subtitle sync.
6. Deliver a private artifact path or approved share target with receipt.

## Capability Map

- Data sources: Google Calendar, Gmail, Slack, Work Board, shared brain notes, or user-provided agenda as available.
- Media: image/video generation, TTS, subtitle timing, short-video assembly, and render QA skills.
- Privacy: redact sensitive meeting names, emails, phone numbers, and private URLs unless the user says they belong in the artifact.

## Safety

- Ask before uploading private schedule, inbox, or chat data to external media providers.
- Never claim a source was checked if it was not reachable.
- Do not send the video externally without explicit approval and a receipt.
