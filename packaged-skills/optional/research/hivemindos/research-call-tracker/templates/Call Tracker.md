# Call Tracker

The research accountability ledger. Every published research call — token, equity, macro, prediction-market — gets a row **at publish time**, with the conviction tier, entry zone, stop, and kill conditions committed before the outcome is known. Managed per the `research-call-tracker` skill.

Status values: `Watching` · `Open` · `Closed (Win)` · `Closed (Loss)` · `Closed (BE)`

## Calls

| Date | Asset | Venue | Conviction | Entry zone | Stop | Target(s) | Status | Outcome % | Thesis (one line) | Kill condition | Verdict file / note |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
