# Business Simulation Operator

Run a business as a realistic simulation inside HivemindOS, then convert approved actions into actual Work Board tasks.

## Contract

This skill produces operating judgment, schedules, tasks, metrics, and rehearsal decisions. It does not spend money, contact customers, publish content, sign contracts, place trades, or mutate external systems without explicit approval and provider receipts.

## Inputs

- Business type, customer, offer, price point, constraints, and current stage.
- Time horizon: one day, one week, one sprint, one quarter, or ongoing cadence.
- Real-world assets the user allows: inbox exports, CRM data, analytics, calendar, website, docs, storefront, product backlog, or market research.
- Output mode: simulation brief, operator plan, Work Board task set, risk review, or daily command center.

## Workflow

1. Frame the business model: customer, promise, acquisition channel, fulfillment loop, money flow, and bottlenecks.
2. Define simulation state: cash, inventory/capacity, pipeline, open obligations, content calendar, product backlog, and unanswered risks.
3. Build an operating day:
   - Morning: triage metrics, urgent messages, customer risk, and blocked work.
   - Midday: one growth move, one delivery move, one systems move.
   - Evening: score outcomes, update assumptions, prepare tomorrow.
4. Convert only approved real actions into Work Board tasks. Use Queen Bee for routing when agent specialization matters.
5. Keep a decision log with assumptions, simulated facts, real facts, and next evidence to gather.
6. Re-run with updated real data when the user supplies it.

## Capability Map

- Planning and routing: Work Board, Queen Bee, scheduler, and visual artifacts.
- Research: current web/search tools, `hive-pulse`, user-provided data, and competitor/public market evidence.
- Writing: use user writing-style skills when producing customer-facing copy.
- Money or customer side effects: require confirmation and use HivemindOS wallet/payment/messaging/provider gates.

## Output

Return:

- Simulation state.
- Top constraints and confidence.
- Today or this sprint's operating plan.
- Work Board tasks to create, with assignee suggestions.
- Actions needing human approval.
- Evidence to collect before the next simulation.
