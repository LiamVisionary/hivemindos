It is preserved for existing users, forks, audits, and historical reference.

- `community-v0.6.1-final` includes only the archive, ownership, and trademark
  notices added directly on top of `v0.6.1`.

  License. Existing license grants are not revoked or rewritten.

  Inc. and is not published here.
- Stable public integration types live in the separate MIT-licensed
  [`LiamVisionary/hivemindos-sdk`](https://github.com/LiamVisionary/hivemindos-sdk)
  repository.
