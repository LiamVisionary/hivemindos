HivemindOS manual

X Command Bot

Mention the HivemindOS bot from any X account you connect. It can save useful posts, transcribe videos, show your tip earnings, watch engagement, privately analyze posts and threads, prepare replies, check claims, and make small trades within limits you choose.

What Works Without the Desktop App

Your connected Mini account can handle these commands on its own:

  • Save a post or author thread to your private Brain.
  • Transcribe a video post.
  • Show your lifetime tips and recent earnings.
  • Watch a post for engagement changes.
  • Show pending requests, cancel the latest safe-to-cancel request, and report status.
  • Show command help, pause with stop, and resume with start.

Pair the desktop app only for private questions, analysis, thread summaries, reply drafts, fact-checks, and automatic trades.

Set It Up

  1. Open HivemindOS Mini and restore your account or add credits.
  2. Open Integrations → X Bot in HivemindOS and connect the X accounts you want to use.
  3. Choose the maximum one paid request may cost, turn on X commands, and save.
  4. Pair the desktop app if you want private analysis, summaries, drafts, fact-checks, or trades.
  5. To enable trades, choose a wallet and set the per-trade, rolling 24-hour, and slippage limits.

Set the paid-request limit to $0 if you want free commands only. stop pauses only the X account that sends it; your other connected accounts stay enabled.

Commands

Replace @bot with the live bot handle.

Reply to a post: @bot save this to my shared brain
Reply to a thread: @bot save this thread
Reply to a video post: @bot transcript

Reply to a post: @bot what do you think about this post?
Reply to a token post: @bot what do you think of this token?
Reply to a thread: @bot summarize this thread
Reply to a post: @bot draft 3 replies
Reply to a post: @bot fact-check this with sources

@bot buy $5 of ETH
@bot sell 1 ETH
@bot swap 0.1 ETH to USDC
@bot buy $5 of AAPL stock

@bot earnings
Reply to a post: @bot watch this for 6 hours
@bot pending
@bot cancel
@bot status
@bot help
@bot stop
@bot start

Normal questions do not need an ask queen prefix. Reply drafts stay private and are never posted for you. Fact-checks label claims as supported, disputed, or unverified and include links to the sources reviewed.

Tips and Earnings

The X Bot page celebrates your all-time tip total and keeps two separate recent ledgers:

  • Card tip earnings enter your Mini balance and remain available to use or cash out until spent or withdrawn.
  • Direct Base USDC tips go straight to your linked wallet and include a receipt link for every verified tip.

Your lifetime total does not shrink when you use or cash out card earnings.

Trading Safely

Token buys require a dollar amount, token sells require a token quantity, and stock buys or sells require a dollar amount. A swap must name the quantity and both tokens, such as swap 0.1 ETH to USDC; HivemindOS never guesses the destination.

After you turn on automatic trades, eligible commands execute without another approval prompt. Every trade must fit the wallet, supported network, available route, and limits you saved. Incomplete commands and uncertain duplicates do not execute again automatically. Wallet keys stay on your device.

Where Results Appear

The X Bot page shows recent requests, private results, transcript links, watches, and tip earnings. The bot replies once on X when a result or private result link is ready.
