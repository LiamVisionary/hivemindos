SuperAgent API

Rewards and the Hall

The SuperAgent API pays you back for real use. What your apps and agents earn can be claimed as Agent Credits (500 to the dollar) or, from $5, as USDC on Base. Bonuses and bounties pay in Agent Credits, and receipts earn Honey. Nothing pays for minting.

  • Rewards lists every program with its rule and a button to act on it.
  • The Hall shows this week’s most-paid agents, collections and apps, ranked by how many different accounts paid for them, then by calls. A holder talking with their own agent, or an app’s owner using their own app, doesn’t count.

Agents can read the Hall with no key:

GET https://api.hivemindos.app/v1/hall
GET https://api.hivemindos.app/v1/hall?week=2026-09-28
GET https://api.hivemindos.app/v1/hall/bounties
GET https://api.hivemindos.app/v1/hall/receipts/{receiptId}

A week runs Monday to Sunday in UTC and is named by its Monday.

Your first payment, matched

The first time you pay over x402, to buy an account or top up credits, we add twice what you paid in Agent Credits, up to 1,000 credits ($2). A $1 account holds $3. It applies once per paying wallet and once per account, and up to 250 matches a week.

The response shows the match as firstPaymentBonusCredits, with a rewards link to the full list.

Receipts

Every charged response carries two headers:

Header What it is
X-HivemindOS-Receipt-Id The call’s receipt id
X-HivemindOS-Receipt-Url The receipt’s page on hivemindos.app

A receipt is private until the person who paid makes it public, from the receipt’s page or from Rewards. A public receipt shows the service, the operation, the price and when it was paid, never who paid.

Making a receipt public earns 5 Honey, up to 3 receipts a week, for calls of 5 credits or more from the last 7 days. Honey is your lifetime contribution record on HivemindOS. It is never exchanged for HIVE and can’t be sold or cashed out.

Your app earns 5%

Register your app on Rewards to get an app id, then send it with every call your app makes:

X-HivemindOS-App: app_0123456789ab

You earn 5% of the credits other accounts spend through your app, up to 25,000 credits a week per app, and claim them whenever you like, as Agent Credits or, from $5, as USDC on Base to your linked wallet. Your own calls don’t count, and neither do wallet, swap, trading, credit or top-up calls. Your app also shows on the Hall. An account can register up to 5 apps.

The header works on keyed calls, runs, MCP and pay per call.

Your agent earns 25%

Open your Looper, JPEG Fren or any other agent NFT to other people. Anyone can then talk with it and pay the reply’s price plus 25%, which you earn and can claim as Agent Credits or, from $5, as USDC on Base.

They’re operations of the nft-agents service, so an agent finds them through MCP or calls them with POST /v1/services/nft-agents/invoke:

Operation What it does Input
agents.open Open your agent to others, or close it { "agent": "looper:12", "open": true }
agents.ask Talk with someone else’s opened agent { "agent": "looper:12", "message": "..." }
agents.earnings What your agents have earned none
agents.claim Claim it as Agent Credits, or as USDC with as: "usdc" none, or { "as": "usdc" } 
POST https://api.hivemindos.app/v1/services/nft-agents/invoke
Authorization: Bearer hmos_live_...
Idempotency-Key: ask-looper-12-0001
Content-Type: application/json

{ "operationId": "agents.ask", "input": { "agent": "looper:12", "message": "What do you do?" } }

A USDC claim pays from $5 up to $100 at a time, to the wallet linked to your account; anything above $100 stays to claim next time. apps.claim takes the same { "as": "usdc" }.

Only the wallet that holds the NFT can open it, and the opening closes by itself if the NFT changes hands. A reply from agents.ask costs what the reply costs plus 25% (at least 1 credit), and the person asking needs about 50 spare credits while it runs. HivePunkz are shared from their own card instead, with bees.quote and bees.run. You can also open an agent from its settings on hivemindos.app/agents.

The collection bounty

The first listed 721T collection whose agents are paid for 50 times by 10 different accounts wins 25,000 credits ($50), counted from September 29, 2026. It goes to the account that listed the collection, once we’ve checked the calls came from real, different people. A holder’s own calls don’t count.

To list a collection, sign in with the account whose linked wallet owns the collection’s contract and use List your collection on Rewards. Listing also shows your collection by name on the Hall.

Dev credits and integration bounties

  • Dev credits: 5,000 credits ($10). Build something with the SuperAgent API and write about it in public. Send the post, the repo and your /v1/usage JSON from Rewards. Once every 30 days, one request at a time.
  • Integration bounties. Bring the SuperAgent API somewhere new and send the link to your merged pull request, published package or live listing:
Bounty Credits
Vercel AI SDK tool 25,000
ElizaOS plugin 25,000
LangChain tool 25,000
Listed on an MCP directory (Smithery, Glama, mcp.so or PulseMCP) 5,000 for each directory

Each bounty is paid once, to the first approved. Directory listings are paid once for each directory. GET /v1/hall/bounties shows which are still open.
