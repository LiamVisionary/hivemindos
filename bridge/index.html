How it works

No new token. Supply unchanged.

This is not a fork or a second token. The Robinhood contract is the official omnichain deployment of $HIVE, paired with the original token on Base through LayerZero's audited OFT standard. Bridging Base → Robinhood locks tokens in the adapter and mints on Robinhood; bridging back burns and unlocks. Beware of copycats — the only official addresses are in the table below.

Hardened beyond the standard

The HIVE bridge adds protections most OFT bridges skip: per-window rate limits in both directions (an attacker cannot drain the lockbox quickly even if the message layer fails), an emergency pause, and a bridge fee that is hard-capped at 1% in the contract itself — currently 0%. Security is verified by two independent DVNs (LayerZero Labs + Nethermind) with every transfer traceable on LayerZero Scan.

Getting started

  1. Add the network. Click "Add Robinhood chain" above — works with MetaMask, Rabby, Coinbase Wallet, and any EVM wallet.
  2. Get ETH for gas. Bridge a little ETH from Base with Across — it supports Robinhood natively. Gas costs are fractions of a cent.
  3. Bridge your HIVE. Use the widget above. Approve, send, and your tokens arrive in about a minute.
  4. Trade on Uniswap. Official swap link coming with the liquidity pool launch.
