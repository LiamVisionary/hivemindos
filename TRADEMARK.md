# HivemindOS Trademark And Brand Policy

HivemindOS source code and documentation are open source under the MIT License.
That license does not grant trademark rights or permission to imply that a fork,
hosted service, token, reward ledger, marketplace, or modified build is official
HivemindOS.

This policy is meant to keep forks, integrations, commercial services, and
community projects honest about what is official and what is independent.

## Reserved Marks

The following are HivemindOS marks:

- `HivemindOS`
- `HIVE`, when used to identify the HivemindOS token, rewards, compute, or
  official ecosystem services
- `Honey`, when used to identify the HivemindOS rewards system
- HivemindOS logos, app icons, badges, and project artwork
- HivemindOS domain names, social handles, marketplace badges, and official
  service names
- Any confusingly similar name, logo, icon, badge, or product identity

## Allowed Uses

You may use the HivemindOS marks to:

- Refer to the original HivemindOS project.
- Link to the official repository, website, documentation, or release notes.
- State truthfully that your work is based on, compatible with, or forked from
  HivemindOS.
- Preserve copyright, license, attribution, and provenance notices required by
  the MIT License.
- Distribute an unmodified official release without suggesting extra
  endorsement, partnership, or ownership.

Examples:

- `Built with HivemindOS`
- `A fork of HivemindOS`
- `Compatible with HivemindOS`
- `This project is not affiliated with or endorsed by HivemindOS`

## Uses That Need Permission

You need prior written permission from the project owner to:

- Use `HivemindOS`, `HIVE`, `Honey`, or a confusingly similar mark as the name
  of your app, company, token, hosted service, marketplace, package, or domain.
- Use HivemindOS logos, app icons, official artwork, badges, or token marks as
  product identity in a commercial product, hosted service, marketplace listing,
  or fundraising page.
- Present a fork, modified build, hosted service, token, reward ledger, compute
  gateway, or marketplace as official HivemindOS.
- Use HivemindOS trade dress in a way that makes users reasonably think your
  product is the official HivemindOS app or service.
- Register names, domains, social accounts, package names, token names, or app
  listings that are confusingly similar to HivemindOS.

## Forks And Modified Builds

You may use the MIT-licensed source code commercially, including in forks,
hosted services, integrations, and products.

If you distribute or host a modified build, you must make it clear that it is
not the official HivemindOS release. In practice, that means you should:

- Rename the app or service.
- Replace the HivemindOS logo, app icon, token marks, official badges, and
  prominent brand artwork.
- Avoid domains, package names, window titles, about screens, reward ledgers,
  marketplace listings, and social accounts that imply official status.
- Keep required license and attribution notices.

You do not need to make every screen visually different. You do need to avoid
confusing users about origin, endorsement, token compatibility, official Honey
rewards, or official hosted services.

## Token, Rewards, And Hosted Services

Only services operated or authorized by the project owner may describe
themselves as official HivemindOS, official HIVE, official Honey, official
HivemindOS compute, official HivemindOS sync, or official HivemindOS marketplace
services.

Forks may run independent ledgers, gateways, marketplaces, sync services, or
cloud offerings, but they must use their own names and must not imply that their
balances, rewards, receipts, tokens, or paid services are official HivemindOS
services.

## Permission

For permission to use HivemindOS marks outside this policy, contact the project
owner through the official repository.

This policy does not limit fair use, nominative use, or other rights you may
have under applicable law.
